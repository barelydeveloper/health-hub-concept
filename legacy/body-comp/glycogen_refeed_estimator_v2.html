Calculation
glycogen lb = glycogen g ÷ 453.59237
associated water lb = glycogen g × water ratio ÷ 453.59237
projected weight = depleted weight + glycogen lb + associated water lb + extra retained water
theoretical BF% = reference fat mass ÷ projected weight × 100
This models body mass, not overnight tissue gain. Food mass, sodium, bowel contents, fluid balance, training, and a scale's proprietary BIA algorithm can push the observed reading outside the estimate.
