Self-checks

Internal consistency tests for units, signs, missing data, and energy double-counting.

    Analytics methodology

    Scale weight is noisy

    Daily weight can move because of water, glycogen, gut contents, sodium, inflammation, and tissue. A single weigh-in is not a tissue measurement.

    BIA is noisy

    Short-term changes in fat or muscle readings should not automatically be interpreted as real tissue change. Hydration and glycogen dominate 24–48 hour BIA swings. Carried-forward BIA values are display-only and do not count as new observations.

    Trends matter more than individual measurements

    Moving averages, EWMA, and regression slopes are used together. Confidence rises when they agree.

    Independent evidence families

    Fat-loss confidence counts distinct families (scale/trend, BIA, waist, energy-balance inference), not correlated copies of the same signal. BIA fat and scale-derived fat are one family. Calibrated Samsung expenditure is not independent of intake vs weight TDEE.

    Nutrition completeness

    Days marked complete (or an intentional fast) get full weight in TDEE calibration. Partial days get little weight. Unmarked days are treated as unknown, not as zero intake.

    Energy balance is an independent signal

    Logged intake versus estimated expenditure implies a tissue-equivalent rate (~3500 kcal/lb). That is a planning heuristic, not a calorimeter. Personal TDEE is an ensemble with a range when more than one source exists.

    Waist is another independent signal

    Waist does not measure body-fat percentage. Agreement among weight, fat estimate, and waist increases confidence that fat is actually changing.

    Forecasts do not peek at the future

    Rolling-origin backtests rebuild the model as of each origin date. Horizons longer than 4 weeks iterate TDEE vs mass weekly. Interval width is widened when historical coverage is poor.

    Confidence is heuristic

    Percentages and probability-style goal figures are model-derived planning aids. They are not statistically validated medical probabilities. Estimates are shown as ~value plus a range when models disagree.

    Clinical anchors

    A DEXA or similar measurement is an independent family. Consensus fat slowly blends toward the latest anchor and fades with age. Energy-balance TDEE is not used to “confirm” the scan.

    Experiments

    Before / during / after comparisons use only days inside each window. Small samples show “Not enough data yet.” There are no p-values.

    Suggested checks

    Decision copy is a short rule list over completeness, disagreement, plateau state, and training volume. High model disagreement blocks aggressive calorie changes. Calibrated Samsung is not treated as independent of weight-reconciliation TDEE.

    Latent-state

    An experimental five-state filter (fat, lean, glycogen, water, transient) runs on fresh observations only. It is shown next to consensus for validation and is not an extra evidence family. Forecasts still start from consensus after the DEXA blend.

    Evidence hierarchy

    Direct observations (scale, waist, lifts) sit above device estimates (BIA, Samsung), then derived trends (EWMA, regression), modeled estimates (TDEE, fat/lean change), then forecasts. Drill-downs label measured vs inferred.

    Training

    Strength logs are separate from Samsung cardio sessions. Best-set is a simple sets × reps × load heuristic.
