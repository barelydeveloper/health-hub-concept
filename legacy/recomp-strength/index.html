Food & Nutrition

?
A 4 fl oz scoop is ½ US cup by volume. I re-audited the built-in catalog against current manufacturer labels and USDA FoodData Central/SR references. Household gram weights are approximate because packing and moisture change the weight of a volume scoop. Brand foods use their label macros; unbranded cooked foods use cooked USDA references. Premier Protein logs one full 11.5 fl oz shake. Tuna is Bumble Bee Solid White Albacore in Water, drained. Kefir uses Lifeway Plain Lowfat; Greek yogurt uses Kirkland Signature Organic Plain Nonfat. The egg entry is 1 large cooked egg.
