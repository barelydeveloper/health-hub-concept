MEASUREMENT NOTE
A 4 fl oz scoop is a volume measurement, not 4 oz by weight. Gram weights are household-measure estimates and will vary with how tightly food is packed and how much moisture remains after cooking/draining. For the most precise real-world tracking, weigh the cooked/drained portion in grams and edit the Food reference if your own consistent scoop differs.
